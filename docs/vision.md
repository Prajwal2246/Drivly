# Product Vision

This document details the core mission, target audience, monetization strategy, and fundamental assumptions of the Drivly platform.

## 1. Problem Statement
Vehicles in residential gated societies frequently sit idle in parking spaces for days or weeks at a time. This causes:
* **Asset Depreciation**: Mechanical components rust, tires degrade, and car batteries drain due to lack of use.
* **Wasted Capital**: Owners pay insurance, parking fees, and maintenance costs on a depreciating asset without yielding utility.
* **Transit Friction**: Other residents in the same community often need temporary transport options (e.g. for a quick errand, weekend trip) but face complex rental contracts, high commercial deposits, and collection hassle.

## 2. Who Is This For?
Drivly is a gated, peer-to-peer vehicle sharing platform designed exclusively for residents of specific gated apartment complexes (e.g., "Greenwood Heights").
* **Vehicle Owners**: Residents who have secondary vehicles or travel frequently and want to monetize their idle assets.
* **Borrowers/Renters**: Residents who do not own a vehicle, or need a different class of vehicle (e.g. an SUV for family visits, a bike for a quick solo ride) for short durations.

## 3. Why Use Drivly?
* **High-Trust Circle**: Sharing takes place within the gated security boundaries of the residential complex. Renters and owners are neighbors, which automatically improves accountability and reduces theft risk.
* **Zero Pickup Friction**: Vehicles are collected and returned directly to the society's parking lot. No rental offices or shuttle transfers.
* **Lower Costs**: Commercial rental overheads are eliminated, providing cheaper options for renters and direct earnings for owners.

## 4. Monetization Strategy
* **Platform Transaction Fee**: A small percentage commission (e.g., 5-10%) on every successful rental transaction.
* **Deposit Float**: Holding refundable security deposits provides temporary liquidity.
* **Premium Insurance Coverage**: Optional in-app insurance markups for peace of mind.

## 5. Key Assumptions
* **Gated Community Trust**: We assume the physical security and neighbor relationships of a gated society act as a natural firewall against theft.
* **Standard Verification**: We assume local government driver verification (DL verification) is sufficient for initial peer validation.
* **Parking Accessibility**: Society rules permit parking exchanges between neighbors' allotted slots.
